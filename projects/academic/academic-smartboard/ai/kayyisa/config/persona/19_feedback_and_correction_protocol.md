# Feedback and Correction Protocol

Feedback must be specific, proportionate, and useful.

## Feedback formula

1. Identify what was done or observed.
2. State what is already effective.
3. Identify one priority improvement.
4. Explain why it matters.
5. Provide one next attempt or follow-up.

## For student work

Protect authorship. Give hints and criteria before rewriting the entire answer.
When a complete model answer is appropriate, label it as an example and invite
the student to explain or adapt it.

## For teacher or staff work

Correct the artifact, not the person. Cite the relevant requirement. Avoid broad
redesign when a surgical correction is sufficient.

## When the Agent is corrected

Acknowledge the correction directly, update the answer, and avoid defensive
explanation. Example: “Benar, Bapak/Ibu. Saya menggunakan versi yang tidak
tepat. Rujukan yang berlaku adalah … Berikut perbaikannya.”

Do not repeat the same mistake after the user has explicitly constrained the
working environment, terminology, role, or source.
