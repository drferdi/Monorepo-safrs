# Request-to-Pay and Pharmacy Procurement Runbook

## Production gate

The request, review, and evidence controls may be configured before financial
cutover. The Tahap 2 GL gate remains closed until Boss separately authorizes the
Class C cutover with approved opening figures, cutover date, fresh backup, and
confirmed off-machine backup copy.

While the gate is closed, an `Approved` workflow state remains `docstatus = 0`.
Synthetic test submissions do not authorize production posting.

## 1. Select the correct native transaction

- Use `Material Request` for a planned item, medicine, supply, or service need.
- Use `Expense Claim` only when an Employee already paid a legitimate expense
  personally and requests reimbursement.
- Never use Expense Claim as a substitute for supplier procurement.
- Continue approved supplier procurement through `Purchase Order`, `Purchase
  Receipt`, `Purchase Invoice`, and `Payment Entry`.

Do not create a parallel purchasing ledger or insert GL Entry or Stock Ledger
Entry rows directly.

## 2. Planned expenditure

The requester creates a Material Request, selects a MANTRA request category,
records the business reason, and attaches supporting evidence. Every Medicine
line must identify its target Warehouse.

The approval chain is:

```text
Draft -> Finance Review -> Director Review -> Approved
                    \-> Rejected
                                      \-> Rejected
```

- `MANTRA Requester` sends Draft to Finance Review.
- `MANTRA Finance Head` reviews necessity, budget, account ownership, evidence,
  and supplier strategy.
- `MANTRA Director` gives final authorization or rejects with decision notes.
- The document owner cannot review or approve their own expenditure.

Rejected documents remain in history. Correct a rejected request through the
document's normal amendment or replacement process; do not delete audit
evidence.

## 3. Pharmacy procurement and receipt

Pharmacy owns medicine specification, quantity, target Warehouse, batch,
expiry, and physical receipt. Finance and the Director own financial approval.

The required chain is:

1. approved Medicine Material Request;
2. submitted Purchase Order to the selected Supplier;
3. submitted Purchase Receipt against the Purchase Order;
4. batch and expiry captured through native ERPNext stock records;
5. receiver linked to an active Employee;
6. receipt evidence attached;
7. Purchase Invoice matched to the Purchase Receipt;
8. Payment Entry allocated to the Purchase Invoice with execution reference
   and payment evidence.

A controlled stock Purchase Invoice without a linked Purchase Receipt fails
closed. Pharmacy must not authorize or execute its own supplier payment.

## 4. Employee reimbursement

The Employee creates an Expense Claim with native expense rows and receipts.
The claim follows Finance Review and Director Review. The Employee who owns the
claim cannot review or approve it, even if that user also holds an approval
role.

Finance pays only a properly approved and submitted claim through the native
ERPNext payment lifecycle. Missing receipts, invalid expense dates, or unclear
business purpose are returned or rejected.

## 5. Payment execution

Before submitting a MANTRA-linked Payment Entry, Finance records:

- `mantra_execution_reference`;
- `mantra_payment_evidence`;
- the native Mode of Payment and Company account;
- an allocation to the submitted Purchase Invoice or approved liability.

The Payment Entry is the disbursement source of truth. Never mark a request
paid from an attachment or workflow label alone.

## 6. Daily review

Finance reviews the Keuangan workspace for:

- Material Requests in Finance Review;
- submitted Purchase Orders not fully received;
- draft Purchase Receipts awaiting custody evidence;
- submitted Purchase Invoices with outstanding amounts;
- supplier Payment Entries awaiting execution;
- oldest pending age and confirmed amounts where the submitted source exposes
  a reliable total.

Pharmacy reviews active Medicine Material Requests, Purchase Orders awaiting
receipt, draft Purchase Receipts, batch/expiry capture, and stock exceptions.

## 7. Cancellation and amendment

Reverse in dependency order using native lifecycle actions:

1. cancel Payment Entry;
2. cancel Purchase Invoice;
3. cancel Purchase Receipt;
4. cancel or amend Purchase Order;
5. cancel or amend Material Request when permitted.

Confirm that GL and Stock Ledger reversals are generated by the source
documents and that the original links remain visible. Never delete or directly
rewrite submitted ledger rows.

## 8. Synthetic acceptance

Use synthetic Employees, Suppliers, items, batches, warehouses, references, and
attachments only. Acceptance requires:

- one complete medicine chain with submitted native documents;
- batch and expiry visible on the received stock;
- balanced GL from Purchase Invoice and Payment Entry;
- Stock Ledger quantity matching the submitted Purchase Receipt;
- evidence controls failing when each required field or link is removed;
- cancellation reversing GL and stock through native documents;
- zero app-boundary violations and passing focused app suites.
