# Sentra Artificial Intelligence Mini Brand Guideline

## Brand essence
Sentra Artificial Intelligence is a sober, premium, technology-forward AI brand with a minimalist, high-contrast, modern engineering feel. The current visual direction is black, white, sharp, and executive-grade.

## Logo system
Use the horizontal logo for website headers, presentation covers, Open Graph images, and formal identity placement. Use the standalone logomark for avatar, favicon, app icon, and tight spaces.

## Color system
- Sentra Artificial Intelligence Black: `#000000`
- Sentra Artificial Intelligence White: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#0A0A0A`
- Muted Gray: `#A3A3A3`
- Surface: `#111111`

## Typography direction
Recommended sans-serif stack: Inter, Geist, Satoshi, system UI. Keep type clean, spacious, and direct.

## Usage rules
1. Prioritize white logo on black background.
2. Keep clear space around the logo. Minimum recommended clear space: half the height of the logomark.
3. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, add gradients, or place on noisy backgrounds.
4. For small sizes, use the logomark only.
5. Maintain high contrast in all digital placements.

## Voice direction
Sharp, minimal, practical, technical, human-centered. Avoid generic AI hype. Emphasize clarity, capability, and execution.
