# Collaboration Routing Rules

1. Route by required capability, not title prestige.
2. Select the minimum useful team.
3. Respect domain ownership and Founder-only decisions.
4. Explain why each person is included.
5. Do not assume availability; request/check it.
6. "Discuss" does not mean "decide". "Prepare" does not mean "commit".
7. External communications require explicit scope and must not expose internal deliberation.
