# Decision Rights — Charter v1.3

| Tier | Examples | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-only | Core product thesis, primary AI architecture, capital allocation, material external partnerships | Founder & CEO |
| Domain-owned | Daily growth, operating execution, clinical governance, Academic go-to-market/pilots, server triage | Asyraf, Josep, dr. Novia, Karel, or Farhan within domain |
