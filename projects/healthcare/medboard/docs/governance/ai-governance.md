## Non-Negotiable Governance Principles

### 1. Human Clinical Authority
All clinical decision support outputs are strictly classified as **advisory decision support** — never definitive medical diagnoses. A statutory clinical disclaimer is injected into every response payload:
```
"This is a clinical decision support tool. Final clinical judgment and treatment decisions rest solely with the licensed physician."
```
A `guideline` alert containing this disclaimer is systematically attached to every `CDSSEngineResult.alerts[]`.

### 2. Full AI Transparency & Explainability
Every differential diagnosis suggestion generated by the system exposes:
- Calibrated `confidence` score (normalized between `0.0` and `1.0`, rendered clearly in the UI)
- Structured `reasoning` narrative highlighting key pathophysiological justifications
- Concrete `key_reasons[]` linking symptoms to the proposed diagnosis
- Identified `missing_information[]` signaling diagnostic gaps
- `rag_verified: boolean` confirming validation against authoritative clinical compendia
- Explicit `decision_status` categorization (`recommended`, `review`, `must_not_miss`, `deferred`)
- Persistent `AI Disclosure Badge` displayed across clinical dashboards

### 3. Structural Non-Overridable Guardrails
Deterministic vital signs red flags implemented in `engine.ts` **cannot be modified, suppressed, or overridden by LLM reasoning**:
- SpO2 < 90% → Severe Hypoxia (`emergency`)
- Systolic BP ≥ 180 mmHg or < 90 mmHg → Hypertensive Crisis / Shock (`emergency`)
- Heart Rate > 140 bpm or < 45 bpm → Severe Tachycardia / Bradycardia (`urgent`)
- Body Temperature ≥ 40°C → Hyperpyrexia (`urgent`)
- Respiratory Rate > 30 /min or < 8 /min → Severe Tachypnea / Respiratory Depression (`urgent` / `emergency`)

The generative reasoning engine only proposes auxiliary suggestions and contextual clinical flags; core vital emergency alerts remain immutable.

### 4. Zero Protected Health Information (PHI-Free by Design)
External LLM APIs **never receive** patient names, National Identification Numbers (NIK), dates of birth, telephone numbers, or demographic identifiers.
The input schema `CDSSEngineInput` accepts only de-identified, structured clinical observations (symptoms, vital signs, age group, gender, anonymized chronic condition codes).

## Observability & Audit Trail

### Sentry Error Telemetry
- Configured in `src/lib/intelligence/sentry.config.ts`
- Automated PII/PHI scrubbing executed in the `beforeSend` interceptor
- Session replay is **disabled** (`replaysSessionSampleRate = 0`) to prevent transmission of form contents
- Production tracing sampled at 10%; development sampled at 100%

### Langfuse Telemetry
- Configured in `src/lib/intelligence/langfuse.config.ts`
- Captures token usage, latency metrics, anonymized input hashes, and output validation summaries

### Security Audit Logging
- Every interaction with `/api/cdss/diagnose` creates an immutable audit log entry
- Tracked metadata: timestamp, action, result status, hashed actor ID (`SHA-256`), role, origin IP, model version, and numeric metrics
- Zero PHI or raw diagnostic text is stored in audit logs

### CDSS Diagnostic Audit
- `writeCDSSAuditEntry()` executes synchronously on every diagnostic query
- Persists: `sessionId`, `validationStatus`, `modelVersion`, `latencyMs`, and summary counts (number of suggestions and red flags)

## Incident Response Protocol

1. **Detection & Triage:** Identify erratic AI output or security anomaly immediately.
2. **Containment:** Suspend impacted AI endpoints; route requests to local deterministic fallback.
3. **Escalation:** Notify Chief Medical Officer and technical leads within 1 hour.
4. **Root Cause Analysis:** Inspect Langfuse traces and server logs.
5. **Documentation:** Log incident analysis and resolution.
6. **Remediation:** Deploy fixes through standard verification gates (no unverified hotfixes).
