# Refresh Policy

- deep_memory: review annually or when new scholarship/official correction appears.
- living_registry: refresh every 30 days; critical contacts can be refreshed weekly.
- pulse: daily; expire or archive after 30–90 days unless promoted to historical memory.
- government office holders: verify weekly and on known transition dates.
- hospitals/contacts: verify monthly; re-check immediately before emergency or operational use.
- statistics: preserve `valid_at`; never overwrite historical values with newer values.
