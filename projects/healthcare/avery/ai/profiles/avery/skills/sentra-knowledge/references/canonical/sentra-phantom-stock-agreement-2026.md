01  PARTIES AND PURPOSE

This agreement is made in Jakarta on 15 August 2026 by and between (i) dr. Ferdi Iskandar, S.H., M.Kn., as Founder, President Director and sole legal shareholder of PT Sentra Artificial Intelligence (First Party), and (ii) Asyraf Hadi, Josep Arianto, dr. Novia Anggraini, Karel Sinatra and Farhan Nugroho, S.T. (together, Second Party). It records a contractual right to conditional economic participation—not legal share ownership.

02  DEFINITIONS

| TERM | MEANING |
| --- | --- |
| Phantom Stock | A contractual, virtual unit evidencing a conditional right to a cash participation payment. It is not a share, option, debt instrument or security. |
| Safe Distributable Profit | Profit remaining after operating costs, payroll, licences, tax, compliance, independent clinical validation and a minimum six-month runway reserve. |
| Mission & Innovation Capital | 32.5% of Safe Distributable Profit retained by the Company for R&D, clinical safety, licences, resilience and public-benefit impact after the 7.5% Karel Sinatra Core Team participation. |
| Vested Units | Units that have completed the applicable cliff and vesting conditions and remain subject to this agreement. |

03  NO OWNERSHIP OR GOVERNANCE RIGHTS

Phantom Stock grants no legal or beneficial share ownership, voting right, dividend right, information right beyond what the Company elects to provide, board seat, management authority, security interest or right to compel a corporate action. The First Party retains 100% legal ownership and operational voting control unless formally changed through a valid corporate act.

04  PARTICIPATION POOL AND ALLOCATION

If and only if a distribution is approved after the Safe Distributable Profit test, the Company applies the Charter v1.3 framework: 32.5% Mission & Innovation Capital, 30.0% Founder participation, 7.5% Karel Sinatra Core Team participation and 30.0% Founding Team pool. Karel’s 7.5% is funded solely from Mission & Innovation Capital; the Founder and Founding Team pool are unchanged. Each Founding Team participant’s payment is calculated from their pool percentage and vested fraction.

Novia Anggraini and Rp36m to Farhan Nugroho, before vesting, withholding and any permitted deferral.

05  VESTING AND CLIFF

Vesting begins on 15 August 2026 and runs for 48 months, subject to continuous active service and compliance with this agreement.

A 12-month cliff applies. If service ends before the cliff, all unvested and purportedly accrued units lapse without payment.

After the cliff, units vest monthly in 1/36 instalments until 100% vested at month 48, subject to the Company’s vesting ledger.

Unvested units lapse immediately when service ends. Vested units do not guarantee payment; payment remains subject to the distribution conditions below.

06  DISTRIBUTION CONDITIONS AND PROCESS

The Founder must determine in good faith that the Safe Distributable Profit test is met and that payment will not impair solvency, legal compliance, clinical safety, tax obligations or committed operating runway.

The Company may retain or defer payment for reserves, dispute review, tax withholding, error correction, customer refunds, regulatory matters or material adverse events.

A written calculation statement will show the period result, approved pool, vesting fraction, withholding and net payment. A participant may submit a good-faith calculation query within 14 calendar days.

No payment is due until formally approved and paid; this agreement creates no fixed salary, guaranteed bonus or dividend entitlement.

07  CONDUCT, CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Participants must protect confidential information, customer data, clinical information, trade secrets, pricing, source code and security materials. Work product created within the engagement scope must be documented and assigned or licensed to the Company as required by the applicable engagement agreement. Material fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breach, IP misappropriation or serious policy breach may result in forfeiture to the extent permitted by law and after fair written notice and review.

08  TAX, RECORDS AND LEGAL STATUS

Payments are intended to be administered as service-related incentive/bonus payments unless a written tax opinion and corporate documentation require otherwise. The Company may withhold and remit taxes, and each participant remains responsible for their own tax filings. The Company will maintain a vesting and payment ledger. This agreement is subject to Indonesian law; any amendment, conversion to ESOP or issuance of legal shares requires separate written corporate and legal documentation.

09  TERM, AMENDMENT AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This agreement remains effective until terminated or superseded in writing. Amendments, waivers and conversions require written approval by the First Party and the affected participant. The parties will first seek good-faith resolution for 30 days; unresolved disputes shall be submitted to the competent courts of Jakarta, without prejudice to mandatory legal remedies.
