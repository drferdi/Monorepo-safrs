01  FOUNDING COMPACT

Sentra begins with people, not titles. Technology extends human judgment and empathy; it does not replace accountable human decision-making. Success is shared fairly. Failure is examined collectively, without scapegoating, to improve the system.

Human first: integrity, accountability and care are non-negotiable.

AI augments everyone: one governed intelligence layer supports each function within approved access boundaries.

Human authority: final judgment, accountability and escalation remain with named human leaders.

Modular resources: the core team owns shared capabilities and uses external specialists for bounded expertise.

05  OPERATING RHYTHM

Weekly Founding Council: 60 minutes maximum—what changed, what is blocked, what needs a Founder decision, and the next three non-negotiable priorities.

Daily domain stand-up: asynchronous where possible; blockers escalated with owner, impact and requested decision.

Monthly operating review: objectives, risk register, cash, service reliability, customer evidence and compliance status.

Incident protocol: protect users first, preserve evidence, appoint an incident owner, communicate facts and complete a learning review.
