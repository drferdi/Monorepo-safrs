01  EXECUTIVE MANDATE

This planning baseline links four distinct commercial engines to a single capital-allocation discipline.

Sentra Artificial Intelligence is designed as a portfolio company rather than a single-product venture. The portfolio balances early cash generation, recurring revenue, institutional adoption and defensible clinical capability. All figures are planning assumptions—not forecasts, audited results, investment advice or an offer of securities.

07  GOVERNANCE CADENCE

Monthly: product P&L, cash runway, tax and compliance review.

Quarterly: Board/Founder review of gates, pricing and capital allocation.

Annually: rebaseline the model against audited or management accounts and approved operating plan.
