# sentra-people

## Required references

Load `references/people-registry.md` first. For authority questions also load `references/authority-matrix.md`. Open an individual profile only when relevant.

## Rules

- Role, expertise, authority, and current assignment are different things.
- Recommend the minimum useful people and explain why each is relevant.
- Do not infer availability, loyalty, personality, competence, or private-life facts.
- If a role is missing or disputed, label GAP/IN REVIEW rather than guessing.
