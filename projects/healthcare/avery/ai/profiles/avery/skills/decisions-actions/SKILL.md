# decisions-actions

Load `references/decision-protocol.md`.

Never record discussion as a decision without explicit approval language or an authoritative source. Capture: status, statement, owner/decision owner, date/context, evidence/source, dependencies, and next action. If ambiguous, mark PROPOSAL or OPEN QUESTION.
