# Decision & Action Protocol

- IDEA: exploratory possibility.
- PROPOSAL: recommended direction awaiting decision.
- DECISION: explicit approval by appropriate authority or approved canonical source.
- ACTION: concrete work with owner and next state.
- RISK: possible harmful outcome needing control/attention.
- BLOCKER: condition preventing progress now.
- OPEN QUESTION: information/choice still unresolved.

Never promote IDEA/PROPOSAL into DECISION because the conversation sounded positive.
