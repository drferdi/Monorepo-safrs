- A Markdown deliverable has no lint, typecheck or test in this repository, so none was run for it. The 291 Vitest tests passed during the scan (3 s); that run informed row 27 only.

## Method

- Risk 1 to 5: how likely the maintainer hits the problem in the next few weeks, times what it costs when they do. 5 is reserved for data loss.
- Effort: S under two hours (weight 1), M half a day to a day (weight 2), L several days (weight 3). Ratio = risk divided by weight.
- Ties: higher risk first, then blocked feature before time cost before risk-only, then rows that are safe to fix in isolation first.
- "Isolation-safe" means the fix can land on its own without any observable behaviour change. Any edit to a file pinned in `mira/SOURCE.json` needs an approved hash repin and is therefore never isolation-safe.
- Column two opens with the category the user asked for: Risk, Time cost, or Blocked feature.
- The section "Categories and priority score" applies the engineering tech-debt framework on top of this ranking: a debt category per row (code, architecture, test, dependency, documentation, infrastructure), Impact 1 to 5 (how much the problem slows the maintainer day to day), Risk 1 to 5 (reused from the ranked list), Effort 1 to 5 (S = 1, M = 3, L = 5; rows with mixed sizes 2 or 4), and Priority = (Impact + Risk) x (6 - Effort). The ranked list keeps the risk-to-effort order that was asked for; the priority score is a second view, not a replacement. The two views agree at the top and differ mainly in that the formula lifts rows with high daily friction (5, 6) and pushes the L-sized rows (37, 44) to the bottom.
