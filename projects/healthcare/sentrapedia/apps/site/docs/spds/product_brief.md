# Product Brief

## Function
Define the product in one reviewable brief.

Sentrapedia website is a bilingual-ready public marketing surface centered on physicians. It presents synthetic workflows, clinical-document examples, FAQ, pricing placeholders, and general contact. It communicates that generated material is draft support and that diagnosis, treatment, and referral authority remain with the physician.
