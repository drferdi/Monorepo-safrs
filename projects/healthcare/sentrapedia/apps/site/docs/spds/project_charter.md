# Project Charter

## Function
Authorize the project and its operating boundary.

## Charter

**Owner:** Gaffer (dr. Ferdi Iskandar).

**Purpose:** Provide a public, reviewable Sentrapedia marketing experience for physicians and institutions while preserving physician authority and avoiding unsupported clinical claims.

**Authorized output:** a standalone static website, local build and preview tooling, synthetic presentation media, FAQ, pricing placeholders, and a fail-closed general contact endpoint.

**Excluded:** clinical decision execution, patient record processing, production deployment, live contact delivery without approval, and coupling to another capsule or monorepo runtime.
