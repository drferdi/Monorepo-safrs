# Benefit Risk Analysis

## Function
Assess communication benefit against foreseeable harm.

Potential benefit is clearer public understanding of Sentrapedia's physician-centered concept. Foreseeable harms are overreliance on marketing examples, disclosure of patient information, or belief that clinical effectiveness is validated. Current controls reduce but do not eliminate these risks. Public release requires claim review, privacy readiness, and license clearance.
