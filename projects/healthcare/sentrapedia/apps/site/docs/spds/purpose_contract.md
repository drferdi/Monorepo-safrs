# Purpose Contract

## Function
Authorize the invariant purpose agents and maintainers must preserve.

## Contract

The site subsystem exists to explain Sentrapedia publicly and demonstrate physician-centered documentation workflows. It may inform and invite general contact. It must not become a clinical decision system, patient-record store, live AI runtime, or production communication service without a new Gaffer decision.

A technically passing change is unacceptable if it weakens physician authority, introduces real patient data, invents evidence or pricing, or couples the site subsystem to the monorepo.
