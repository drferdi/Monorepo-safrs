# Clinical Safety Plan

## Function
Guide prevention of harm from healthcare marketing content.

Review every clinical claim, example, and interaction for automation overclaim, missing physician review, patient-data solicitation, and misleading validation language. Use synthetic cases only. Treat a report that content influenced unsafe care as a high-priority content incident and remove or correct the claim before further publication.
