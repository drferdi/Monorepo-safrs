**Status:** Editorial proposals. Review product facts and approve before publication.  
**Primary audience:** Indonesian healthcare teams, institutional partners, researchers and product visitors.  
**Language preference:** Bahasa Indonesia first; English for technical clarity or international audiences.

## Editorial red lines

**Do:** "Dirancang untuk membantu alur kerja klinis" (designed to support clinical workflows).  
**Do not:** "Terbukti meningkatkan keselamatan pasien" unless supported by documented evidence.

**Do:** "Eksplorasi riset" / "research direction".  
**Do not:** "Tervalidasi secara klinis" unless validation has been formally documented.

**Do:** "Pintasan menuju produk" / "gateway to products".  
**Do not:** describe the Sentraverse marketing site as diagnosing or treating patients.

## Tone checklist

1. State what exists today, and separately what is planned.
2. Use one clear promise per headline.
3. Prefer clinicians and system outcomes over abstract AI buzzwords.
4. Translate specialist terms when an Indonesian plain-language term is available.
5. Add disclaimers only where materially necessary, with product-owner review.
6. Verify privacy, regulatory and performance claims before publishing.
