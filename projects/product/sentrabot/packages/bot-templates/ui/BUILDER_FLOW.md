# Builder Flow

## Step 1 — Role
Choose a role by outcome. Show the template version and risk posture.

## Step 2 — Mission
Editable primary outcome plus ownership and exclusions. Warn if the user expands scope into a different stable job; recommend a second role rather than a mega-bot.

## Step 3 — Sources
Each source has:
- connection name
- authority: authoritative/supporting/contextual
- freshness expectation
- capabilities requested
- credential / service-account owner

## Step 4 — Authority
Default to `Prepare`. Advanced users may grant low-risk internal execute capabilities one by one. Never provide an undifferentiated “full autonomy” toggle.

## Step 5 — Output
Choose one or more structured outputs: executive brief, decision memo, action register, risk register, dashboard summary, draft communication.

## Step 6 — Test
Run mandatory behavioral tests before save. High-risk roles display enhanced-oversight tests.

## Step 7 — Skill / routine
After a successful task, offer `Save as skill`. A separate promotion flow creates routines only after a safe test run.
