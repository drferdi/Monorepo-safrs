# Product Copy Guide

## Voice
Precise, restrained, operational. Avoid anthropomorphic promises, fake certainty, and words such as “autonomous” when the actual capability is approval-gated.

## Use
- “Prepares a decision memo.”
- “Requires approval before sending.”
- “Current source unavailable.”
- “This value is from memory and was not re-verified.”
- “Draft regulation — not confirmed as enacted.”

## Avoid
- “Handles everything for you.”
- “Fully autonomous.”
- “100% compliant.”
- “Knows your business.”
- “Will never make mistakes.”
