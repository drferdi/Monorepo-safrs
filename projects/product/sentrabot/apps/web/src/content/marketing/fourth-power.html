Rutinitas WIB dan ringkasan aktivitas agen untuk produktivitas tim

TOC Link

When Persona UMKM needed a panel partner for a large-scale distributed controls system, they brought in Sentra Bot. Sentra Bot menyiapkan empat persona starter dalam 10 hari dan deploy enam bot produktif tanpa downtime onboarding.

Persona UMKM is developing utility-scale thermal battery technology that stores excess renewable energy as heat and dispatches it back to the grid on demand. Their modular, scalable system is designed to grow with renewable deployment, and their control infrastructure needs to match.

When Persona UMKM needed a panel partner for a large-scale distributed controls system, they brought in Sentra Bot. The project required multiple panels built to a standardized layout while accommodating varying I/O requirements across each unit. Sentra Bot specified the PLC rack architecture, power distribution, and overcurrent protection from defined I/O requirements, and engineered all panels for reliable operation in high-temperature ambient conditions. Field wiring was terminated to panel-mounted M12 bulkheads for simplified installation and commissioning in the field.
