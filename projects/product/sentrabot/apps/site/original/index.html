No, Cora will never send emails for you. It drafts responses in your voice that appear in your drafts folder, but you always have full control. You review, edit if needed, and decide when to send. Think of it as having a thoughtful assistant who prepares options for you, not someone who speaks on your behalf.

When you first sign up, Cora analyzes a selection of your email history to understand your patterns—who you respond to quickly, what types of emails you typically act on, and your communication style. It learns that emails from your boss or key clients are probably important so you always see them. You can also chat with Cora to adjust its understanding over time.

You can always see emails that Cora archives by going into the “Next Brief” label that it creates, so you’ll know you never miss anything. If it briefs something important, you can chat or email with it to tell Cora not to do that again

Yes, currently Cora works with Gmail and Google Workspace. Outlook and other email providers are not supported yet.

A Brief is a beautiful, scannable summary delivered twice daily (morning and afternoon) containing all your non-urgent emails—think newsletters, notifications, calendar invites, and FYIs. It's designed to be read in 30 seconds instead of spending hours in your inbox. It’s delivered as an email, and you can read it on the web.
