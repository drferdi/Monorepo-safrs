# KEDIRI — A Living Civilization
## Visual Design System & Art Direction Bible

**Version:** 0.1  
**Design objective:** **One civilization. Many eras. One unmistakable visual identity.**

---

# 1. Design Thesis

The experience must not look like a government portal with old photos, an online catalogue, a fantasy Java game, a luxury-brand microsite with history pasted on, or a random collection of GSAP experiments.

It should feel like:

# A CIVILIZATION EXPERIENCED THROUGH TIME.

Two visual layers operate together:

## Constant layer
Brantas · earth · darkness · recorded language · material · time · human presence.

## Era layer
Color · light · material · geometry · typography behaviour · image treatment · pace · spatial composition.

The visitor should feel a new century without feeling a new website.

---

# 2. Design Character

## Monumental
Dates, inscriptions, bridges and turning points have physical presence.

## Scholarly
Metadata and evidence treatment communicate research discipline.

## Cinematic
Composition and light guide attention like film.

## Human
History returns to workers, families, writers, students, merchants and communities.

## Contemporary
The interface is visibly made in 2026; it does not impersonate an ancient manuscript.

---

# 3. Civic DNA

Kediri civic colors are treated as identity DNA, not wallpaper. Green/yellow/gold references may appear at civic moments such as 879, republican identity, navigation, finale and Djojo ing Bojo.

Official crest artwork must always use official supplied files and prescribed colors. The project palette below is a digital art-direction system, not a legal replacement for official identity specifications.

---

# 5. Era Color Script

## Ancient / 879–1042
Charcoal · copper · stone · deep land green · restrained gold.

Approximate visual ratio: 70% darkness / 20% material / 10% illumination.

## Panjalu
Obsidian · royal indigo `#1B2140` · burnished gold `#C8A04C` · warm stone.

Royal means symmetry, scale and restraint—not ornament overload.

## Collapse 1222–1293
Charcoal · conflict crimson `#851F27` · ash · oxidized gold.

Red acts as pressure, not full-screen wallpaper.

## Memory / folklore
Black · smoke · ritual red · manuscript brown · muted violet-black.

The viewer should feel an epistemic change, not merely another section.

## 17th-century conflict
Parchment · military earth · black ink · dark river.

## Colonial / industrializing
Paper ivory · iron · oxidized copper · cane green `#68714C` · steam grey.

## Occupation / revolution
Near-monochrome + documentary red `#9C2626`.

## Industrial city
Tobacco brown `#7B5036` · steel `#59605E` · industrial red `#99362F` · carbon.

## Connected / modern
Sky `#AFC8D2` · concrete `#D2D1CB` · River Deep · Civic Green · Civic White.

## Finale
Natural Kediri color returns. The palette resolves rather than explodes.

---

# 6. Accessibility Color Rule

Body text must satisfy WCAG AA contrast. Large cinematic imagery never excuses unreadable text.

Approved approaches over imagery:

- intentional empty photographic space;
- local gradient field;
- solid editorial plate;
- controlled layered composition.

Text shadow is not a rescue strategy.

---

# 7. Typography System

## Source Serif 4
Narrative voice, historical titles, hero statements, literary emphasis.

## Geist Sans
Modern interface, body copy, navigation, controls, contemporary display.

## Geist Mono
Evidence, archive metadata, inventory numbers, coordinates, technical dates.

## Unicode-compliant Javanese typeface
Use only for actual meaningful script content and after linguistic review—not as decorative pseudo-ethnic styling.

---

# 8. Type Hierarchy

### Monumental Time
`clamp(6rem, 18vw, 16rem)` approximately. Dates may behave as architectural objects.

### Era Title
`clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 8rem)`.

### Scene Title
`clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 5rem)`.

### Narrative Lead
20–28px desktop, limited line length.

### Body
18–20px desktop; 16–18px mobile; line height 1.5–1.65.

### Archive Label
11–13px mono; uppercase only when short.

Narrative reading width normally stays around 520–680px even on wide screens.

---

# 9. Typography Motion Rule

Use the lowest split granularity necessary.

- lines: default editorial narrative;
- words: controlled rhythm;
- characters: rare signature moments such as KADHIRI / PANJALU JAYATI.

Do not turn every heading into character choreography.

---

# 10. Material System

## Water
All eras. Continuity, boundary, movement, life, military geography, urban geography.

## Copper
879. Record, authority, durability.

## Stone
Epigraphy, land, memory.

## Gold
Panjalu. Royal authority and illumination; small areas of intensity.

## Ink
Literature, Panji, maps, documents. Can transform into route, line, abstraction.

## Paper
Literature through administration. Preserve real material when source is real.

## Iron
1869 onward. Engineering, structure, modernity.

## Film / photographic emulsion
Late colonial, occupation, revolution. Grain should arise from the source medium, not a global vintage filter.

## Steel
Modern industry. Cleaner and more repetitive than colonial iron.

## Air / glass
Dhoho and contemporary era. Openness, reflection, distance.

---

# 11. Material Transitions

Preferred causal transitions:

- water → copper;
- copper → stone;
- gold → ink;
- ink → fracture;
- paper → map;
- map line → iron line;
- iron → film;
- film → steel;
- road line → runway line.

Material transformation is preferred over generic wipe/fade transitions.

---

# 12. Lighting System

## Ancient
Raking, low-angle, localized. Material is discovered from darkness.

## Royal
Warm, controlled, central, hierarchical.

## Literary
Soft amber, intimate, close to paper and hand scale.

## Conflict
Hard-edged, uneven, occasional restrained red bounce.

## Colonial
Natural daylight + paper white; harder neutral engineering light where appropriate.

## Revolution
Documentary and source-driven.

## Industrial
Repetitive practical lights, machine highlights, horizontal windows.

## Modern
Natural, clear, airy.

The site should gradually become visually cleaner/brighter toward 2026.

---

# 13. Darkness Rule

Darkness must communicate distance, incomplete evidence, pressure or focus—not simply “premium cinematic website.”

At least major documentary/modern sections should become substantially light. Contrast gives darkness meaning.

---

# 14. Grid / Composition

## Desktop
12-column editorial grid; 4–6vw margins; narrative usually 4–5 columns; metadata 2–3 columns; hero visuals may break grid.

## Tablet
8-column conceptual system.

## Mobile
4-column; one dominant narrative column; full-width imagery; no squeezed desktop composition.

---

# 15. Composition Modes

## Monumental
One object, one message, large negative space. Use for 879, Panjalu Jayati, Dhoho, finale.

## Editorial
Documents, maps, context, evidence, administration.

## Immersive
Airlangga, bridge construction, industrial transformation.

Not every GSAP scene should be immersive.

---

# 16. Era-Specific Composition Grammar

- Ancient: asymmetrical object in darkness; large empty field.
- Panjalu: central axis, more symmetry.
- Fall: off-axis, cropped, unstable.
- Folklore: layered, floating, less architectural.
- Colonial: grid, measurement, horizontal systems.
- Revolution: documentary crops, irregular rhythm.
- Industrial: repetition, module, network.
- Modern: wide, open, sky, reduced density.

---

# 17. Hero Object Rule

Every hero scene has exactly one dominant visual idea:

879 inscription; 1042 divided landscape; 1135 Panjalu Jayati; 1157 manuscript; 1222 fracture; 1293 three-force convergence; 1869 bridge; 1912 elevation; revolution machine; 1958 network expansion; 2024 runway; 2026 city+river.

If reviewers cannot name the hero object, composition is unfocused.

---

# 18. Archival Treatment

Preserve original aspect ratio, tone, grain and material character.

No default decorative vintage frame. No default sepia. No indiscriminate colorization.

Captions must be able to expose date, creator, institution, inventory and evidence type.

Zoom should reveal evidence, not merely create drama.

For signature imagery:

1. show whole image;
2. allow viewer to recognize it as real evidence;
3. move into meaningful detail;
4. show metadata/context;
5. transition without erasing source integrity.

---

# 19. Map System

Three map languages must stay distinct:

1. **Historical map** — actual historical cartography.
2. **Reconstruction map** — modern scholarly/interpretive graphic with uncertainty.
3. **Modern map** — current GIS/wayfinding.

Ancient boundaries use zones/gradients unless exact lines are supported.

---

# 20. Evidence UI

Evidence status uses shape + label + color:

- `● PRIMARY RECORD`
- `■ HISTORICAL FACT`
- `◐ INTERPRETATION`
- `◇ TRADITION`
- `✦ FOLKLORE`

Color is supplementary; the written label is always visible.

Evidence drawer should feel like a concise museum catalogue, with “supports” and “does not establish” fields.

---

# 21. Navigation Art Direction

Navigation visually recedes during storytelling and becomes fully visible on intent/focus.

Desktop concept:

`KEDIRI` left; `Timeline · Explore · Sources · Sound` right.

Timeline looks like a temporal instrument, not an ecommerce slider.

---

# 22. Photography Art Direction — Modern Kediri

Avoid generic tourism tropes: everything at golden hour, empty landmarks, posed officials, oversaturated HDR.

Shoot lived Kediri: eye-level urban life, human scale, documentary color, real skin, natural contrast.

Landmarks get both documentary and cinematic coverage.

The Brantas gets the broadest emotional range.

---

# 23. Crop / Safe Zone System

Master acquisition remains uncropped. Derivatives should support 16:9, 4:5, 3:2, 1:1 and 9:16 as required.

Every hero image stores:

- subject box;
- text-safe region;
- mobile crop;
- tablet crop;
- edge-extension permission.

Do not leave historically important crops to blind `object-fit: cover`.

---

# 24. Surface / UI Rules

- no global glassmorphism house style;
- minimal shadows;
- corner radius generally 0–8px, not SaaS pills everywhere;
- 1px hairline borders where needed;
- grain only when material/medium supports it;
- no global vignette;
- blur is depth, not prestige;
- particles only when materially justified;
- no fake royal ornament / fantasy Java icon set.

---

# 25. Royal Art Direction

Do not create a fantasy palace, gold throne overload, glowing statues or fake historical crowns.

Panjalu dignity comes from:

# BLACK · INDIGO · GOLD · STONE · INSCRIPTION · TEXT · SPACE.

---

# 26. Literary Art Direction

## Bharatayuddha
Structured, courtly, ink, manuscript, text-to-world transformation.

## Panji
More lyrical, migratory, flowing across geography and manuscript traditions.

---

# 27. War Art Direction

War is not red screen + camera shake.

Use broken geometry, direction, absence, maps, documents and real archive. Violence is communicated as disruption, not spectacle.

---

# 28. Colonial Art Direction

Pair engineering with labour, municipal planning with inequality, factory with worker, infrastructure with extraction. The design may acknowledge engineering beauty without producing colonial nostalgia.

---

# 29. Industrial Art Direction

Show systems, not only machinery: workers, roads, homes, money, transport, data, urban expansion. The visual metaphor is **network**.

---

# 30. Government Presence

Official attribution is visible but restrained: opening/project attribution, sources/partners, footer and finale. Do not stamp the government crest over every cinematic frame.

Quality is the primary form of respect.

---

# 31. Project Lockup

Recommended:

**KEDIRI**  
# A LIVING CIVILIZATION  
**879 — 2026**

Modern typography. Historical material does the historical work.

`DJOJO ING BOJO` should be rare and protected, primarily for the finale and important civic identity moments.

---

# 32. Interaction Visual Tone

Microinteractions: fast, quiet, precise. No bouncy archival UI. Cinematic motion belongs to history; interface remains dependable.

Buttons are text-forward: `Explore Evidence →`, `Open Timeline →`, `View Source →`.

Custom cursor optional desktop-only, never necessary.

Sound control small and non-distracting; default `SOUND OFF`.

No fake prestige loader.

---

# 33. Responsive Art Direction

## Desktop
Cinema + museum installation.

## Tablet
Interactive documentary.

## Mobile
Premium historical graphic novel: large type, strong photos, sequential frames, short parallax, minimal pins, native vertical reading.

Mobile is redesign, not shrink.

---

# 34. Accessibility Art Direction

Accessibility is part of the design system:

- visible focus;
- semantic hierarchy;
- text resizing;
- alt text;
- reduced motion;
- captions/transcripts;
- color-independent meaning;
- touch targets;
- meaningful reading order.

Reduced motion becomes a strong **digital exhibition version**, not a degraded experience.

# 36. Art-Direction Review Gate

Before implementation each scene must pass:

- HISTORY — visual does not overstate evidence;
- HERO — one dominant image;
- ERA — period is felt without reading date;
- KEDIRI — not a generic history site;
- COMPOSITION — attention is controlled;
- TYPE — immediately readable;
- MATERIAL — texture has meaning;
- COLOR — narratively justified;
- LIGHT — supports historical condition;
- MOTION — adds historical meaning;
- MOBILE — native composition exists;
- ACCESSIBILITY — complete without motion/color.

---

# 37. Visual Signatures

1. huge dates interacting with real materials;
2. Brantas as recurring spatial line;
3. real evidence surrounded by cinematic restraint;
4. mono archival metadata;
5. one strong sentence instead of paragraphs over images;
6. material transitions;
7. rare meaningful gold;
8. uncertainty shown visually;
9. increasing light toward the present;
10. stillness after major movement.

---

# 38. Final Principle

At every design review ask:

> **If all animation stopped, would this still be a beautifully designed, historically responsible experience?**

If yes, GSAP can make it extraordinary. If no, the design is not finished.

# REAL HISTORY IS THE PREMIUM MATERIAL.

Kediri does not need to be made cinematic. Our job is to reveal what is already there.
