# KEDIRI — A Living Civilization
## Cinematic Scene Architecture · 879–2026

**Version:** 0.1  
**Experience type:** Scroll-directed cinematic historical journey  
**Motion standard:** Sentra-GSAP v1.0.0  
**Principle:** **Motion in service of historical meaning.**

---

# 1. Experience North Star

The visitor should not feel “I am scrolling through a history website.” The visitor should feel:

> **I entered Kediri in 2026, travelled through more than eleven centuries of memory, and returned to the same city understanding it differently.**

The experience sits between interactive documentary, digital museum and cinematic title sequence.

The protagonist is Kediri itself.

---

# 2. Master Visual Metaphor

# THE BRANTAS IS THE THREAD OF TIME.

Its role changes by era:

- 879 — landscape and continuity;
- 1042 — political boundary and mythic symbol;
- Panjalu — geography of power;
- 1678 — military obstacle;
- colonial era — transport / engineering problem;
- 1869 — crossed by iron;
- 1912 — a force infrastructure must answer;
- revolution — strategic corridor;
- modern era — urban axis;
- 2026 — living continuity.

By the finale, the visitor should intuitively understand:

> **The river has watched everything.**

---

# 3. Global Motion Law

Every animation must answer:

> **What historical idea does this movement communicate?**

If there is no answer, remove the animation.

Examples:

- Airlangga division → landscape separates.
- Panjalu Jayati → scattered authority consolidates.
- Bharatayuddha → writing becomes world.
- Ganter → visual order fractures.
- colonial industrialization → movement becomes measured and mechanical.
- 1869 → bridge assembles from engineering lines.
- 1912 → bridge physically rises.
- revolution → the same machinery changes purpose.
- Gudang Garam → a small footprint becomes a network.
- Dhoho → horizontal transport line becomes runway and flight.

---

# 4. GSAP Production Rules

Binding Sentra-GSAP principles:

- native scroll first;
- no smooth-scroll dependency by default;
- one coordinated scene normally owns one master timeline;
- timeline first, ScrollTrigger second;
- scene owner owns cleanup;
- desktop, tablet, mobile and reduced-motion choreography are distinct designs;
- semantic static baseline exists before motion;
- avoid unnecessary WebGL, filters and heavy paint effects;
- motion may enhance meaning but may not carry exclusive factual meaning.

---

# 5. Master Structure

The journey is organized into approximately 10 Acts and 26–27 production scenes, with 12 protected hero scenes and supporting interludes.

The exact count can be normalized during implementation, but chronological and narrative hierarchy must remain intact.

# ACT I — THE LAND REMEMBERS

## Scene 01 — 879: The First Mark

**Hero object:** Prasasti Kwak  
**Evidence:** primary record + civic commemoration

Near-black void. Macro material surface. Raking light reveals inscription texture. Date appears in stages:

**27 · JULI · 879**

Copy explains the commemorative role precisely.

Master line:

# BEFORE THERE WAS A KINGDOM CALLED KEDIRI, THERE WAS ALREADY A RECORD.

Do not animate characters as if their writing order is known. Do not place the object in a fake Kediri excavation site.

Transition: inscription character / geometry → next epigraphic surface.

---

## Scene 02 — 921: Kadhiri

**Type:** Supporting  
**Hero:** the word KADHIRI

The purpose is one word emerging from history.

# KADHIRI

The modern spelling **KEDIRI** may appear faintly behind it for a controlled temporal echo.

No decorative pseudo-Javanese typography.

---

## Scene 03 — 1015: The Name Endures

Short chronological interlude.

879 → 921 → 1015 align, then move into depth.

Because Carama remains under verification, no fake artefact hero is allowed.

---

## Scene 04 — 1042: The River Divides a Kingdom

**Type:** Hero  
**Evidence:** historical reconstruction + tradition

Topographic East Java landscape. Political separation unfolds.

Labels:

**PANJALU**  
**JANGGALA**

Then evidence mode visibly shifts to **TRADITION**.

Mpu Bharada appears symbolically, not as a realistic authenticated historical person. Sacred water becomes a visual metaphor for the later tradition.

Master line:

# HISTORY REMEMBERS A DIVISION. LEGEND REMEMBERS SACRED WATER.

Signature transition: follow the Brantas. Gold enters the palette.

---

# ACT II — PANJALU RISES

## Scene 05 — Daha: The Centre of Power

Daha is suggested, not falsely reconstructed.

Use river, landscape, material fragments, structural silhouettes and controlled incompleteness.

Copy explicitly acknowledges that the complete ancient cityscape is not known with certainty.

Master line:

# SOME CITIES SURVIVE AS RUINS. DAHA SURVIVES THROUGH TEXT, PLACE, AND MEMORY.

---

## Scene 06 — 1135: Panjalu Jayati

**Type:** Hero  
**Hero:** inscription + typography

Full black.

Small:

**1135**

Then:

# PANJALU
# JAYATI

Gold, but restrained.

Signature motion: letters distributed across depth gradually align into a single phrase, communicating political consolidation.

No fake palace battle.

---

## Scene 07 — 1157: Words Become Monuments

**Type:** Hero  
**Hero:** manuscript / text

Old Javanese writing moves across the surface. *Bharatayuddha* emerges. Characters leave the page and become abstract spatial conflict—not literal cinematic armies.

Master lines:

# TEXT BECOMES WORLD.

# KINGS RULED THE PRESENT. POETS REACHED THE FUTURE.

Transition: manuscript sheets / ink routes into Panji transmission.

---

## Scene 08 — Panji: A Story That Left Kediri

Map of Southeast Asia. Manuscript routes move like ink, not military conquest.

The scene is about cultural transmission, later manuscripts and the afterlife of a Kediri-Janggala story-world.

Master line:

# THE KINGDOM HAD BORDERS. ITS STORIES DID NOT.

---

# ACT III — THE THRONE BREAKS

## Scene 09 — 1222: Ganter

The royal geometry from Panjalu begins stable and symmetrical. Cracks appear. The visual centre of gravity moves away.

Names enter as historical anchors:

KERTAJAYA · BRAHMANA · TUMAPEL · KEN AROK · GANTER

Master line:

# KEDIRI DID NOT VANISH. POWER MOVED ELSEWHERE.

No photorealistic battlefield fabrication.

---

## Scene 10 — 1292: The Return

Black. Ember. **1292**. **JAYAKATWANG**.

Fragments of the former political world reassemble imperfectly. The scene should visually communicate temporary restoration.

---

## Scene 11 — 1293: The Last Kingdom

**Type:** Hero

Three vectors:

**JAYAKATWANG**  
**YUAN**  
**RADEN WIJAYA**

Use strategic movement, map logic and documentary-symbolic visual treatment rather than fantasy Mongol stock warriors.

Final:

# THE KINGDOM OF KEDIRI ENDED.

Pause.

# KEDIRI DID NOT.

---

# ACT IV — AFTER THE KINGS

## Scene 12 — Jayabaya After Jayabaya

Split visual logic:

### Historical Jayabhaya
Inscription · 1135 · Panjalu.

### Prophetic Jayabaya
Later manuscript and cultural tradition.

A visible temporal divide prevents the two from collapsing into one historical claim.

Hero question:

# DID JAYABAYA PREDICT THE FUTURE?

Then reframe:

> Why did later generations repeatedly speak through his name?

---

## Scene 13 — The Shadow Archive

Optional, non-linear cultural room:

- Calon Arang — TRADITION
- Kelud / Lembu Suro — FOLKLORE
- Kediri Curse — URBAN LEGEND / FOLKLORE

This room may be visually freer because the evidence mode is explicit.

Master line:

# FOLKLORE IS NOT FAILED HISTORY. IT IS EVIDENCE OF WHAT PEOPLE REMEMBER, FEAR, AND IMAGINE.

Return control must clearly restore chronology.

# ACT V — EMPIRES CROSS THE BRANTAS

## Scene 14 — 1678: The River as Fortress

Strategic map. Brantas becomes a military obstacle.

Master line:

# SAME RIVER. DIFFERENT WAR.

This deliberately rhymes with 1042.

# ACT VI — IRON, SUGAR & THE MODERN CITY

## Scene 15 — Sugar Changes the Land

Begin with agricultural terrain. Survey lines, factory systems, rail/road geometry and industrial organization gradually overlay the landscape.

Core concept:

# LAND BECOMES SYSTEM.

PG Pesantren / Meritjan evidence should be real where available.

Transition: transport line approaches Brantas and stops—the river becomes the next engineering problem.

---

## Scene 16 — 1869: Iron Across the Brantas

**Type:** Hero  
**Potential top-three scene**

Blueprint grid. Engineering geometry draws the actual bridge structure. Line becomes iron. Historical photography becomes present-day survival.

Date:

# 18 MARCH 1869

Then precise comparison:

> Fourteen years before Brooklyn Bridge opened.

No implication that the bridges are the same design type.

Signature transition: camera passes under the bridge into colonial urban geometry.

---

## Scene 17 — 1906: A City on Paper

Colonial map, administrative plan, civic documents, railway and urban systems.

Simultaneous columns:

**MUNICIPAL MODERNITY**  
**COLONIAL INEQUALITY**

Master line:

# MODERN GOVERNMENT ARRIVED. MODERN EQUALITY DID NOT.

---

## Scene 18 — 1912: Kediri Lifts a Bridge

**Type:** Hero micro-spectacle  
**Blocked from exact final choreography until archival verification**

Real bridge geometry. River / measurement context. Scroll-controlled raising to approximately 1.5 m only after exact evidence is locked.

Master line:

# THE RIVER CHANGED. ENGINEERS MOVED THE BRIDGE.

---

## Scene 19 — The People Between the Monuments

Pacing reset. Faces, markets, workers, schools, religious communities, traders, family archives.

Objective: prevent the whole site from becoming king → bridge → company.

This scene depends heavily on expanded archive and community acquisition.

---

# ACT VII — OCCUPATION & REVOLUTION

## Scene 20 — 1942: The World War Arrives

Palette drains. Documentary treatment. No generic Japanese image may be silently presented as Kediri.

If contextual material from elsewhere is ever used, it must be explicitly labelled as contextual.

---

## Scene 21 — 1947–1948: From Sugar to Weapons

**Potential hero; evidence gate required**

Use the same mechanical movement previously associated with sugar production. If the Meritjan conversion claim is verified, the machine's output changes from commodity production to wartime manufacture.

Master line:

# THE MACHINES REMAINED. THEIR PURPOSE CHANGED.

Exact 50 mm / 90 mm visuals remain forbidden until direct evidence is approved.

---

## Scene 22 — 1950: City of the Republic

Three dates align:

**879** — commemorative historical anchor  
**1906** — colonial municipality  
**1950** — republican local government

Master line:

# AN ANCIENT NAME. A COLONIAL MUNICIPALITY. A REPUBLICAN CITY.

Transition: legal-document material into industrial paper / tobacco context.

---

# ACT VIII — THE INDUSTRIAL CITY

## Scene 23 — 1958: From 1,000 m²

**Type:** Hero

Begin small. One building footprint. The system grows into workers, roads, production, neighbourhood relationships and an urban-economic network.

The company is not the protagonist. Kediri's transformation is.

Master line:

# INDUSTRY DID NOT SIMPLY ENTER KEDIRI. IT RESHAPED THE CITY.

---

## Scene 24 — 1990+: From Kediri to the Market

Two simultaneous truths:

### PROSPERITY
jobs, production, capital, national scale.

### CONCENTRATION
dependency, regulatory exposure, structural risk.

Data visualizations must expose source, year, unit and definition. No unsupported 70.5% company-specific claim.

---

# ACT IX — THE CITY CONNECTS

## Scene 25 — Two Bridges, Two Centuries

Jembatan Lama and Jembatan Brawijaya in one frame. Historical and contemporary images align where possible.

Master line:

# INFRASTRUCTURE BECAME MEMORY.

Transition: road line travels west and becomes runway geometry.

---

## Scene 26 — 2024–2026: From River to Runway

A single horizontal line begins as timeline, then becomes runway marking.

Official modern milestones appear only after current official verification.

Historical rhyme:

Brantas → road → iron bridge → rail → runway.

Master line:

# KEDIRI GREW BESIDE A RIVER. NOW THE REGION CONNECTS THROUGH THE SKY.

---

# FINALE — 2026

Return to the same modern Kediri perspective as opening.

Dates cascade. Then:

# 2026

One number remains:

# 1,147

Caption precisely as years since the historical date commemorated as Hari Jadi.

After an entire experience of camera movement, **movement stops**.

People continue moving naturally inside the city.

Final sequence:

# THE NEXT CHAPTER HAS NO DATE YET.

# KEDIRI

### DJOJO ING BOJO

No loud replay CTA. Give the ending space.

# Typography Roles

1. **TIME** — giant dates as spatial architecture.
2. **HISTORY** — readable modern editorial narrative.
3. **ARCHIVE** — compact technical metadata and evidence labels.

Never place long essays over active animation.

# Final Creative Review Questions

For every scene:

- Is the historical claim supportable?
- What is the one dominant hero image?
- Why does the scene exist narratively?
- What meaning does the movement add?
- Can the scene be understood without animation?
- Does the effect justify its cost?
- Is the mobile treatment independently designed?
- Is reduced motion complete?
- Does the evidence classification remain visible where necessary?

If a scene cannot answer these, it is not ready.
