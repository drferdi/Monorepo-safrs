# ASSET EPISTEMIC INTEGRITY

Standing section. Opened after the full inspection of `project-images/` on 2026-08-28.

**Scope of the audit.** All 30 files in `project-images/` were opened and viewed directly — 27 scene assets, one colour variant, and two legacy files. This is not a sample.

**Directory correction.** `apps/web/editorial-preview/journey/` holds webp derivatives of these same PNGs, not independent artwork. Verified perceptually: mean absolute pixel difference between each PNG and its derivative is 3.1–4.0 at 768×512, i.e. compression noise. The findings below therefore describe the production assets themselves.

**Chief decision 2026-08-28 (final).** Do not regenerate. Do not modify the approved image set. Do not stop Batch A. Assets are directed, not replaced. These entries exist so the defects stay visible and are handled by art direction and DOM layering rather than silently shipped.

**Governing rules.** Authority Rules 1–3 in `00_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORITY.md`.

## REDO-ASSET-001
- **Scene:** 01 — 879 — The First Mark
- **Problem:** Pseudo-script; baked date line reading `27 [pseudo-glyph] 879`; stone material against a canon record written on metal.
- **Correction to earlier report:** the middle glyph is an invented character standing in for "Juli", not the numeral 1. Verified at 4× zoom. The earlier reading of `1879` was wrong.
- **Resolution:** Directed, not regenerated. Baked date box measured at `(370,645)-(1240,765)`. Crop window `x=0 y=0 w=1536 h=620` (desktop) and `x=0 y=0 w=1500 h=620` (mobile) keep it out of frame at every supported viewport. Pseudo-script cannot be cropped without destroying the subject and remains as texture. Year and date are DOM.
- **Verification:** `apps/web/tests/framing-baked-text.test.ts` — 15 assertions, passing. Proven to fail when a window is loosened.
- **Status:** READY_FOR_REVIEW
- **Before evidence:** Full-resolution inspection 2026-08-28; 4× zoom of the date line.
- **Reviewer decision:** —
