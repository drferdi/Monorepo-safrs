# Engineering

## Purpose

This document defines the engineering philosophy used throughout the project.

## Core Principles

* Simplicity
* Maintainability
* Consistency
* Reusability
* Verifiability

## Guidelines

Prefer simple solutions that are easy to understand and maintain.

Reuse existing patterns whenever possible.

Avoid unnecessary abstractions, speculative features, and large unrelated refactors.

Before implementation, evaluate complexity, maintenance cost, and long-term impact.

Verification is part of implementation, not an optional step.

## Expected Behavior

Recommend engineering solutions that reduce technical debt, improve reliability, and remain sustainable over the lifetime of the project.
