# Sentra Artificial Intelligence - Brand Guidelines v1.0

**Status:** PRODUCTION / REVIEW

## 1. Identity
The approved logomark is the sole master symbol. Use the supplied SVG assets; do not recreate it. The formal corporate name is **Sentra Artificial Intelligence**. **Sentra AI** is a secondary compact lockup only.

## 2. Logo versions
- Primary: black on light surfaces.
- Reversed: white on #0D1117 or sufficiently dark surfaces.
- Icon-only: product UI, favicon, app icon, social avatar.
- Full horizontal lockup: primary corporate use.
- Short horizontal lockup: constrained UI only.
- Stacked lockup: square/narrow placement only.

## 3. Clear space
Minimum clear space is **1X** on every side, where X equals the approximate band width of the master logomark. More space is preferred.

## 4. Minimum sizes
- Icon: 16 px digital / 6 mm print.
- Full corporate lockup: 180 px digital / 30 mm print.
- At very small sizes use icon-only rather than compressing the full name.

## 5. Color
Core colors: #0D1117, #FFFFFF, #000000. Primary accent: #5B8CFF. On dark surfaces use #5B8CFF; on white surfaces use `#315FD6` for normal-sized text links.

## 6. Typography
Primary: **Geist Sans**. Technical/data contexts: **Geist Mono**. Do not substitute decorative futuristic fonts into the corporate wordmark.

## 7. Do not
Do not stretch, rotate, skew, redraw, recolor the master mark as default blue, add drop shadows, glow, gradients, 3D, bevels, outlines, or place it on low-contrast backgrounds.

## 8. Handoff
Developers should use SVG/currentColor assets and token files. Marketing should use curated PNG/SVG exports, not reconstruct the logo in Canva or other tools.
