The visual root is Swiss / International Typographic Style. Corporate document typography is Archivo for display and major hierarchy, IBM Plex Sans for sustained reading, and IBM Plex Mono for metadata and structured information. Geist is not part of the Corporate Document System typography. No separate blue-led corporate-document language is introduced.
